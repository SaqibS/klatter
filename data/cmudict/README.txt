The upstream commit identifier is not available for this snapshot; its content
hash pins the source. Keep LICENSE.txt with binary distributions.

Cargo validates the raw source and generates a sorted, first-reading lookup
table in OUT_DIR. Only ASCII letters and apostrophes are retained in keys;
AH0 is mapped to AX0. The raw source retains alternate readings and comments.
Do not maintain or edit a second pronunciation table. Local pronunciation
choices belong in built-in or caller-owned overrides.
